Questions Before You Book?

Here are answers to the most common things people ask before getting in touch.

Is the strategy call really free?

Yes, completely. There's no sales pitch at the end — just an honest assessment of your business and what's possible. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you.

How quickly can you get started?

After your strategy call, most clients have a proposal within 48 hours. Once approved, we typically begin building within one week and go live within 2–3 weeks.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

That's exactly what the call is for. You don't need to know anything about AI or automation — just tell us where your time goes and what's frustrating you. We'll figure out the rest.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Most of our clients are small-to-medium service businesses — 2 to 50 employees. You don't need a big tech budget to benefit from automation. We build to your scale.
